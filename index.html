Semester fee collection and registration: SOP

Applies to all universities and all semesters. Owner: Central Team. T0 is the official semester registration start date. Each university’s current phase is shown in the Universities tab.

  • Universities must publish semester dates and fee collection timelines by T-60.
  • All universities must follow the approved timeline without deviation unless approved by the Central Team.
  • Standard communication templates provided by the Central Team must be used for all student and parent outreach.
  • All updates, commitments, and exceptions must be recorded in the central tracker.
  • Any escalation or exception must be logged on the same day.
TermDefinition
T-Day (T0)Official semester registration start date
Paid StudentComplete semester fee paid and verified by finance, Eligible for registration
At-Risk StudentStudent with outstanding dues requiring intervention
Written ConfirmationPayment commitment received via WhatsApp, email, or SMS

Roles and responsibilities

Fee collection and registration are centrally governed and must be run the same way at every university.

COS / Success Coach

• Follow up on fee payments
• Record payment commitments
• Flag at-risk students
• Support registration activities

Program Manager (PM)

• Owner for the university registration and fee collection process
• Makes in-class announcements on fees and registration timelines
• Gathers timelines and mode of payment, and supports students in the payment process

Finance Team

• Verify payments
• Generate payment links
• Apply late fees where applicable
• Share consolidated fee reports

Central Team

• Own the fee collection process
• Issue communications
• Manage access control and exceptions
• Monitor compliance across universities

Exceptions and records

Exceptions. Only the Central Team can approve an exception. Universities on ground team cannot grant access, waive the late fee or mark attendance for an unpaid student on their own.

Records. Keep for every student: payment method, committed date, written confirmation, every follow-up date and outcome, and any exception approval.

How statuses are worked out, and where the data comes from
Open
Today is on or after the fee collection start date and on or before the deadline.
Upcoming
The fee collection start date is after today.
Closed
The deadline has passed.
Dates pending
No exact start or end date yet (“Yet to decide”, “3rd week of Dec”, blank).
Check dates
The deadline is before the start date, usually a wrong year.
No announcement sent
Open, or opening within 30 days, with every channel in the tracker blank.
Sources
The fee sheets, trackers and contacts are loaded into the database by the import script. You only see the universities linked to your email.
